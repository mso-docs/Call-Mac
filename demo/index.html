If your browser asks to access local devices, allow it for this site. If access is blocked, check site permissions or try a current desktop Chrome/Edge browser. You can also run this demo locally.

        
Ollama can stay on localhost. You do not need to open ports or expose it to the internet.

      

      

        Where does my information go?
        
Your browser sends the problem, feedback, and selected experience directly to the Ollama address you choose. This site has no AI proxy, accounts, analytics, or shared database. The hosting provider still serves the page and may record ordinary access logs.

        
Conversation and connection settings stay in this tab's memory. Reloading clears them. Nothing is saved to local storage. Avoid entering passwords or other secrets. AI advice can be wrong; review it before acting.
